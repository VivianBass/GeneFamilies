Definitions of groups:
	Conserved Orthologs
	Definition: Conserved orthologs are highly conserved orthologous genes found across different species. Following an analysis with Orthofinder, the best ortholog is selected for each species-gene pair, ensuring that each gene in one species has only one corresponding ortholog in another species. This group represents the most reliable and evolutionarily conserved orthologous relationships.
		
	In Paralogs
	Definition: In paralogs are duplicated genes within the same species that are not directly related to a conserved ortholog. These genes arise from recent duplication events and do not belong to the groups of conserved orthology. They are specific to the species in question and have no ortholog in another species.

	Special In Paralogs
	Definition: Special in paralogs are duplicated genes within the same species that do have a relationship with a conserved ortholog. This classification as a special variant of in paralogs suggests a possible functional or structural conservation.
	
	Out Paralogs
	Definition: Out paralogs are duplicated genes in different species that have no relationship with a conserved ortholog. These genes represent duplications that predate speciation and are not conserved in the main orthologous group.

	Special Out Paralogs
	Definition: Special out paralogs are duplicated genes in other species that do have a relationship with a conserved ortholog. They represent duplications that occurred in different lineages but have retained a relationship with the conserved ortholog genes.
