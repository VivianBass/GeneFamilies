
### **Experimental Conditions**

- **Experiment**: *test_diet_P_&_M_filtered*

    - **Objective**:
        - Analyze **Conserved Orthologs** vs. **In-paralogs**.
        - Compare **Special Out Paralogs** with other gene groups.
        - Perform statistical tests (T-tests and Wilcoxon) on expression differences.
        - Generate and analyze distance and angle distributions.

- **Species Comparison**:

    - RNA-seq data from *Drosophila melanogaster*, *Drosophila simulans*, and *Drosophila sechellia*.

    - **Generalist**: *D. melanogaster* (dietary flexibility).
    - **Specialist**: *D. sechellia* (specialized on Morinda fruit).
    - **Intermediate**: *D. simulans* (dietary behavior not as flexible).

- **Dietary Treatments**:

    - **M Diet**: Medium-composition (balanced control).
    - **P Diet**: Protein-enriched diet.

- **Tissue Samples**:

    - **Muscle**: High metabolic activity.
    - **Whole body**: Organismal response to diet.
