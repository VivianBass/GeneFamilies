## **EasyVectorOmics-Pipeline**


- The **EasyVectorOmics** Pipelinefacilitates bioinformatics analysis by enabling streamlined data handling, statistical computation, and visualization for comparative gene expression studies. 

- the *EasyVectorOmics - Pipeline* is divided into three sections:

      - **Section 1**: Data Loading
      - **Section 2**: Distance Computations
      - **Section 3**: Visualization and Statistical Computations


#### **Section 1: Data Loading**

- **Data Loading**: Efficient preprocessing and loading of gene expression data, gene groups, and gene families using R scripts.  

- **Scripts**:

  - *load_gene_expression_data.R*: Handles RPKM data loading.
  - *load_gene_groups_data.R* : Imports ortholog and paralog relationships.
  - *load_gene_families_data.R* : Processes MCL output for gene family clustering.

- **Normalization**: Adjusts raw expression data using RPKM methodology to ensure comparability.

      - **Purpose of Normalization**: Corrects technical biases, such as sequencing depth and RNA quality, ensuring fair comparison of gene expression levels across samples.  
      - **Key Methods**: Common approaches include RPKM (Reads Per Kilobase Million), FPKM (Fragments Per Kilobase Million for paired-end reads), and TPM (Transcripts Per Million, providing consistent cross-sample scaling).  
      - **RPKM Overview**: Adjusts gene expression by normalizing for gene length and total read depth, enabling accurate sample-to-sample comparison.  
      - **Length Normalization**: Divides raw read counts by gene length in kilobases, preventing longer genes from artificially inflating expression levels.  
      - **Depth Normalization**: Scales read counts to a million mapped reads, accounting for differences in sequencing depth across samples.  
      - **Benefits**: Produces comparable expression values, reduces technical bias, and facilitates more accurate differential expression analysis.  

  - **Overview - Input Data**:

#### **Section 2: Distance Computations**

- **Distance Computation**: Advanced pairwise metrics, including Euclidean distances, cosine angles, and angle-to-diagonal alignment measures for comparative analysis.  

- **Scripts**:

  - *compute_exp.prof.dists.R* : Calculates Euclidean distances.
  - *compute_exp.prof.dists_statistics.R* : Summarizes distance statistics.
  - *exec/compute_angles_to_diagonal.R* : Computes cosine angles for expression vectors.


#### **Section 3: Visualization and Statistical Computations**

- **Statistical Analysis**: Comprehensive tests like t-tests, Wilcoxon tests, and descriptive statistics to identify significant differences in gene expression patterns.  
- **Visualization**: Versatile plotting functions for gene expression distributions, annotated statistical results, and tissue-specific explorations.  

- **Scripts**:

  - *exec/plot_angles_to_diagonal_distribution.R* : Visualizes angle-to-diagonal alignment
  - *exec/plot_exp.prof.dists_angles_distributions.R* : Cosine angle visualizations
  - *exec/plot_exp.prof.dists_euclidean_distributions.R* : Euclidean distance visualizations
  - *exec/plot_exp.prof.dists_euclidean_tissue_distributions.R* : Tissue-specific distance visualizations

- **Statistical Tests**:
  - Parametric t-tests and non-parametric Wilcoxon tests compare orthologous and paralogous gene expression distributions.
  - P-values adjusted using Benjamini-Hochberg correction.
