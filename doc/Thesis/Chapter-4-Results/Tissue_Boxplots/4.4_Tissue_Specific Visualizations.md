### **Overview:**  

This distribution boxplot illustrates the tissue-specific Euclidean distance distributions, calculated for each of the four tissue-condition combinations: **all muscle under diet M**, **all muscle under diet P**, **whole body under diet M**, and **whole body under diet P**. The Euclidean distances were computed pairwise between the expression profiles of genes within each gene group. Distances were calculated using two approaches—regular expression profiles and log2-transformed expression profiles—and are presented separately for each tissue-condition combination. For each combination, the results are displayed as four boxplots showcasing mean and median statistics analyzed through t-tests and Wilcoxon tests. Statistical comparisons, including annotated significance levels, provide insights into gene group variations under tissue-specific and condition-specific contexts. Key findings were extracted for further interpretation.
