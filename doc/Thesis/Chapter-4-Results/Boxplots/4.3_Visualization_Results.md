The figure consists of four boxplots in a single frame: Median (t-test), Mean (t-test), Median (Wilcoxon), and Mean (Wilcoxon). - *Overview*:   **Overview:**  
This distribution boxplot illustrates the distance distribution of Euclidean distances, calculated in two ways: using regular expression profiles and log2 expression profiles. The pairwise Euclidean distances were computed between the expression profiles of genes within each gene group, and the resulting distances are presented as distributions for each gene group. Annotated significance levels are displayed directly on the plots, providing insights into the statistical comparisons. Key results were extracted from the boxplots for further interpretation.

- *- Descricption of results:*

**Description of Results:**

1. Regular Expression Data Analysis:
- The con_orthologs_v group shows the highest mean values (25.47) and median values (25.74), indicating greater expression distances
- The in_paralogs_v and out_paralogs_v groups show notably lower mean values (13.38 and 14.15 respectively) and medians (20.76 and 18.82)
- The special_in_paralogs_v and special_out_paralogs_v groups have similar mean values (23.52 and 24.57) to con_orthologs_v
- Gene count distribution is uneven:
  * con_orthologs_v, special_in_paralogs_v, and special_out_paralogs_v each have 166 genes
  * in_paralogs_v and out_paralogs_v have significantly fewer genes (9 and 6 respectively)

2. Log2 Expression Data Analysis:
- The transformation to log2 scale reveals similar patterns but with compressed value ranges
- con_orthologs_v maintains the highest mean (0.26) and median (0.27)
- in_paralogs_v and out_paralogs_v show the lowest means (both 0.16)
- The difference between mean and median values is less pronounced in log2 scale
- Gene counts remain consistent across both analyses

3. Key Observations:
- in_paralogs_v and out_paralogs_v groups have 9 and 6 genes respectively, while other groups each contain 166 genes
- con_orthologs_v shows distance values around 25.47 (regular) and 0.26 (log2)
- special_in_paralogs_v and special_out_paralogs_v display similar distance patterns
- The log2 transformation results in values between 0.16 and 0.27 across all groups
- Mean and median values within each group show small differences in both regular and log2 data
