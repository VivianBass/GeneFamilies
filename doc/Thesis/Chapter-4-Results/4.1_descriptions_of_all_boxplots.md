## **allgemeine Beschreibung aller plots**

*Address By Hypothesis/Research Question:*

Main Research Question: How does the mean distance between orthologous genes compare to that of paralogous genes, and what implications does this have for understanding evolutionary relationships and functional divergence in gene families?

`Sub-questions:`

        What happens to gene expression after duplication?
        What happens to gene expression after speciation?


- _Distribution Plots_

        - purpose of these plots, 
        - whatd they show, 
        - what you can analyse with them,
        - how are they created ?


*Experiment or Analysis:*

Vector Space Analysis of gene expression data
Comparative genomic analysis between species
Expression profile comparison using distance metrics
Statistical analysis of gene expression divergence

*Methods used to get the results:*
EVO Pipeline consisting of:
OrthoFinder for gene family identification
RNA-seq data processing workflow
R-Pipeline for expression analysis
Distance measurements and statistical testing
- Euclidean Distances
- Angular distances (Cosine angles), transform radians to degrees
- Tissue-specific Euclidean distances
- Angle to diagonal describe

*Parameters:*
Expression values: TPM (Transcripts Per Million)
Distance metrics: Euclidean distance, cosine angles, tissue-specific Euclidean distance
Statistical tests: t-tests, Wilcoxon rank-sum tests
Multiple testing correction: Benjamini-Hochberg method for FDR control

*Gene Groups:*
5 Gene Groups:
con_orthologs: Genes orthologous between species from speciation
in_paralogs: Paralogs from lineage-specific duplications after speciation
specific_in_paralogs: Specialized subset of inparalogs
out_paralogs: Paralogs from duplications before speciation
specific_out_paralogs: Distinct subset of outparalogs

*Filtered by:*
Coding sequence (CDS) regions only
Quality-controlled RNA-seq data
Expression level thresholds

*Means:*
Individual means calculated for each of the 5 gene groups
Overall mean across all groups
Statistical summaries including mean and median distances
Error measurements and statistical significance markers

- mean and median statistics were calculated on the euclidean distances for each gene family and also apllyed for each gene group
- so that we would only have 1 values per gene family for the euclidean distances, representing the mean and also the same for the median

*Expression Profiles:*
Regular expression profiles:
Raw TPM values
Tissue-specific expression patterns
Biological condition variations
Expression profiles log2:
Log2-transformed TPM values
Normalized expression data
Scaled for better comparison across conditions

*3 Frames:*
Regular: Raw expression values in TPM
Log2: Log2-transformed expression data
