
## 1.2. **Reference Study: Gene Expression Analysis in Cardamine Hirsuta**

        A. Study Overview
        B. Relevance to Current Research


- _Cardamine hirsuta_ genome project.
- [DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/nplants.2016.167](https://doi.org/10.1038/nplants.2016.167)


## A. **Study Overview**

Research Strategy
- Comparative genomic study between C. hirsuta and A. thaliana, combining high-quality genome assembly with transcriptome analysis to understand genetic basis of trait diversity

Key Discoveries
- Identified transcription factors (PLT5/7) and tandem gene duplications (PMEIs) as primary drivers of morphological evolution, particularly in leaf and seed dispersal traits

Evolutionary Insight
- Gene families under positive selection show increased functional diversity while maintaining domain conservation, suggesting a balanced mechanism for trait diversification within evolutionary constraints



## B. **Relevance to Current Research**

Data Collection Protocol
- RNA extracted from standardized samples (3 leaf replicates, 2 fruit replicates) under controlled conditions for both species using RNeasy Plus Micro Kit and Superscript VILO

Expression Analysis
- RNA-seq data processed using Tophat and HTSeq, focusing only on CDS regions (excluding UTRs) to ensure reliable cross-species comparison
